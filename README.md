A one-line comedy entry about a smart appliance that knows its owner too
well. The fridge does the shopping on its own initiative -- milk and eggs
are expected -- but the third item on the order gives the game away: a gym
membership. The appliance has audited a year of 2 a.m. snack runs and
decided, on the evidence, that this is no longer a shopping list. It is an
intervention, staged by the one witness who sees every late-night visit
and never sleeps.

## The entry

> My smart fridge just ordered milk, eggs, and a gym membership. It has seen my 2 a.m. snack history and staged an intervention.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.
